HOW TO PLAY

Simple rules, deep puzzles.

Stop on rocks

Swipe to slide; it stops when it bumps into something.

Holes and ladders

Fall through a hole to the floor below. 2- and 3-floor stages too.

Fish break rocks

Eat a fish to break the next rock you hit with your beak.

3 stars, fewest moves

Clear in the fewest moves for 3 stars. Oni Mode and Time Limit Mode too.
