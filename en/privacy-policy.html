Privacy Policy

iPhone app "Slide Out, Penguin!"

This policy explains how Slide Out, Penguin! (the "developer") handles information about users of the iPhone app "Slide Out, Penguin!" (the "app"). If this English version and the Japanese version differ, the Japanese version prevails.

1. Information the app itself keeps

The app has no accounts or sign-in. The developer does not collect your name, email address, phone number or other personal information.

Your progress (cleared stages, stars, fewest moves), coins, tickets, outfits and settings are stored only on your device and are never sent to the developer or third parties. Deleting the app deletes them.

2. Information sent for advertising

To keep the app free, it shows ads through Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To show ads, Google's SDKs built into the app (Google Mobile Ads SDK and User Messaging Platform SDK) may send the following to Google:

  • Device identifiers such as the advertising identifier (IDFA) — the IDFA only if you allow it in section 5
  • Your ad consent status (checked by Google's consent tools)
  • Approximate location estimated from your IP address
  • Information about the ads shown and your interactions with them (taps, video plays)
  • Information about app launches and use
  • Device model, OS version, and app performance and crash information

The developer does not receive this information or use it to identify you. The developer only receives aggregated numbers from Google, such as ad impressions and revenue.

If you buy "Remove Ads", the app does not run the ad SDKs and sends nothing for advertising.

3. Downloading news and stage information

To get news and up-to-date stage information (such as the number of stages), the app downloads one configuration file from the developer's website on GitHub Pages (https://penguinslide.github.io/), for example when it starts. As part of how the internet works, your IP address and device and app type are sent to GitHub, Inc., which hosts the site. The developer does not receive this information or use it to identify you. This download also happens if you bought "Remove Ads".

4. How the information is used

Information sent to Google is used to:

  • show ads, including ads tailored to your interests
  • measure ad performance and prevent ad fraud
  • find and fix problems in the ad service

Google explains how it handles information on these pages:

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy?hl=en
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites?hl=en
  • Google advertising policies: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads?hl=en

5. Allowing tracking, and how to change it

The first time you open a stage, the app asks through Apple's App Tracking Transparency whether you allow tracking with the advertising identifier (IDFA). If you don't allow it, every feature of the app still works the same; ads are just not tailored to your interests.

You can change this at any time in:

  • the iPhone Settings app → Privacy & Security → Tracking → すべペン

You can also manage Google ad personalization in Google's My Ad Center (https://myadcenter.google.com/?hl=en).

6. In-app items and purchases

The app's coins and tickets are earned by clearing stages. They cannot be bought with money and have no cash value.

The app offers a one-time purchase, "Remove Ads". Purchases and payments are handled by Apple's App Store; the developer never receives your payment details (such as card numbers). The app checks whether you own the purchase through Apple's systems.

8. Children

The app is suitable for a wide audience including children, but it is not made specifically for children. The developer does not collect personal information from anyone, including children. Parents can turn tracking off as described in section 5, and can manage app use with Screen Time on iPhone.

9. Changes to this policy

The developer may update this policy when the app's features or the law change. Updates are posted on this page with a new revision date. Important changes are also announced in app update notes.
